1 Overview

Electricity Bill Splitter Pro ("the App", "we", "our") is a utility app designed for PG owners and landlords to manage electricity bills and tenant payments. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy describes what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

2 Information We Collect

A. Data You Provide (Stored Locally Only):

  • Property name and landlord name
  • Tenant names and room numbers
  • Phone numbers (for WhatsApp sharing only)
  • UPI ID (for bill message generation only)
  • Electricity meter readings and billing amounts
  • Payment records and billing history

Yes - All of the above data is stored exclusively on your device using browser localStorage. It is NEVER uploaded to any server.

B. Automatically Collected Data:

  • The App itself collects NO usage analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers.
  • Third-party advertising (Google AdMob) may collect limited ad-related data as described in Section 4.

3 How We Use Your Information

  • Bill Calculations: Meter readings are used to calculate electricity bills for each tenant.
  • WhatsApp Sharing: Phone numbers are used solely to open WhatsApp with a pre-filled bill message. No data is sent to our servers.
  • Local Backup: You can export all your data as a JSON file to your device storage.

We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal data to third parties (except as described in the Advertising section).

4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

This App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising platform operated by Google LLC.

Warning - Google AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized ads. This includes device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data.

AdMob's data collection may include:

  • Advertising ID (GAID) of your device
  • IP address (used for general location)
  • Device type, OS version, and app usage data
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)

For more information on how Google uses advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use AdMob

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your device's Settings - Google - Ads - Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5 App Permissions

  • INTERNET: Required to load advertisements (AdMob) and optional cloud fonts. No personal data is transmitted.
  • READ/WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE: Used only when you manually export or import your JSON backup file.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile feedback on button interactions (optional).

6 Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored on your device only. You have full control:

  • Delete all data: Open App - More - Settings - Reset All Data
  • Delete app data: Android Settings - Apps - Bill Splitter Pro - Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app - all local data is permanently deleted

7 Children's Privacy

This App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us to have it deleted.

8 International Users & GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to delete your data
  • Right to opt out of personalized advertising

Since all personal data is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly by using the app's built-in reset feature or uninstalling the app.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.
